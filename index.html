Hobbies

Cycling

I remember the thrill of riding my bike down the steepest hill in our street for the very first time.

My friends and I would race each other until the sun went down, and I almost always lost.

Drawing

I once filled an entire sketchbook with drawings of dinosaurs in a single weekend.

My mom proudly stuck my best drawing on the fridge, where it stayed for years.

Toys

Lego

I built a giant castle that took up half of my bedroom floor.

Stepping on a stray Lego brick at night was painfully unforgettable.

Toy Cars

I had a shiny red race car that I believed was the fastest in the world.

I would line up all my cars in a perfect row before sending them crashing into each other.
